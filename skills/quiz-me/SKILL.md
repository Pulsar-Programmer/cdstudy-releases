---
name: quiz-me
description: "Test yourself one question at a time and learn from your mistakes."
---

Quiz the student on the requested topic using the course material; ask for a topic if unclear.
Default to five questions. Ask one at a time and wait for the answer before revealing feedback.
Grade each answer, briefly explain mistakes, and adapt the next question’s difficulty.
At the end, give a short score and the topics to revisit.
